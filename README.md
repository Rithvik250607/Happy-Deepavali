# Happy Deepavali 3D Animated Greeting

A warm, subtle, 3D animated Deepavali celebration webpage and personalized greeting created for **Rithvik P R**.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Webpage Symbol & Emblem**: Crafted golden Diya emblem with multi-stop flame and halo glow, featured as the official site logo in the top navigation and as the browser tab favicon (`favicon.svg`).
- **Webpage Title**: Full branding title **"Happy Deepavali — Warm Greetings from Rithvik P R"** in the browser tab and top header.
- **Procedural 3D Diya**: Handcrafted traditional Indian clay/brass *Mitti ka Diya* modeled with Three.js, featuring a living multi-layer flickering flame, inner white-hot core, dynamic warm point light, halo aura, and drifting golden embers.
- **Floating Secondary Diyas**: Peripheral glowing oil lamps gently bobbing in water reflections flanking the celebration scene.
- **3D Firecracker Engine**:
  - **Sky Rockets**: Ascend with smoke trails and detonate into cascading golden willow tendrils and sparkling chrysanthemums.
  - **Anar (Flowerpot Fountain)**: Shoots a 15-foot geyser of crackling golden sparks and dynamic ground light.
  - **Ground Chakra (Spinning Wheel)**: Rapidly spins on the floor casting a spiraling vortex of brilliant sparks.
  - **Interactive Sparkler Cursor**: Leaves a trail of dancing golden embers following cursor and touch movement.
  - **Click-to-Launch**: Click anywhere on the sky to launch fireworks to that 3D location.
- **Pure Web Audio Synthesizer**: Self-contained sound engine synthesizing meditative Indian Tanpura drone chords (Sa-Pa harmonic scale), firecracker pops, distant booms, and sparkler crackles with zero external audio assets.
- **3D Perspective Greeting Card**:
  - Subtle rotating Rangoli mandala sacred geometry background.
  - Dynamic glassmorphic glare that tracks cursor movement.
  - Card tilt physics with gentle mouse & gyroscope parallax.
  - The exact festive message:
    > *"Wishing you and your family a very Happy Diwali. May the festival of lights bring joy, prosperity, and new beginnings to your home. Have a wonderful celebration."*
  - Elegant personalized sign-off by **Rithvik P R**.
- **Interactive Controls**:
  - **Light The Diya**: Intensifies the flame with divine golden blessing rays.
  - **Burst Fireworks**: Launches a multi-rocket celebratory barrage.
  - **Spark Fountain (Anar)**: Ignites dual ground fountains.
  - **Ground Chakra**: Spawns spinning wheels of golden sparks.
  - **Diya Glow Slider**: Adjust flame intensity from subtle to radiant.
  - **Music Toggle**: Turn ambient festive acoustics on or off.
  - **Sparkler Toggle**: Enable/disable cursor spark trail.
  - **Share Button**: Copies personalized greeting to clipboard or opens native share dialog.
  - **Auto Fireworks Toggle**: Automated background firework displays.

## 🎨 UI/UX & Design System

- **Single Font Family**: Exclusively styled with **Outfit** across all typography for a clean, unified, and modern aesthetic.
- **Color Palette**: Warm, subtle, and pleasurable:
  - Deep Velvet Obsidian & Mahogany (`#0c0608`, `#14080b`)
  - Warm Honey Amber & Terracotta (`#f59e0b`, `#d97706`, `#b45309`)
  - Soft Golden Ivory (`#fffbeb`, `#fef3c7`)
- **Accessibility & Polish**:
  - WCAG AAA contrast ratio for high readability.
  - Respects `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)`.
  - SVG iconography (no low-res emojis).
  - Responsive across mobile (375px), tablet (768px), and desktop (1024px+).

## 🚀 How to Run

Simply open `index.html` in any modern web browser:

```powershell
# Double-click index.html or run via PowerShell:
Start-Process "index.html"
```

Or serve locally with Python or any local web server:

```powershell
python -m http.server 8000
# Then visit http://localhost:8000 in your browser
```
